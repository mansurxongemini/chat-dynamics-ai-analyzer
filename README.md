A specialized system architecture and prompt engineering framework for qualitative conversational dynamics analysis. Utilizes structured **IRAC** (Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion) methodologies to analyze multi-party and dyadic dialogue logs.

---

## 🌟 Analysis Vectors

1. **Attachment & Initiative Tracking**: Measures pacing, latency asymmetry, and initiative balance.
2. **Social Hierarchy & Group Interaction**: Identifies central conversational nodes, passive interaction patterns, and engagement distribution.
3. **Sentiment & Semantic Subtext**: Analyzes formal vs. informal register shifts, evasive responses, and boundary setting.
